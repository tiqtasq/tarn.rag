# Protecting tanks from corrosion

A large steel tank loses metal to corrosion over its service life. Two complementary defenses slow
this down: a protective internal coating that isolates the steel from the stored product, and a
cathodic protection system that suppresses the electrochemical corrosion of the tank bottom.

## Coating inspection

Inspect the internal coating for blisters, holidays, and disbondment. A holiday detector locates
pinholes in the film before they expose bare steel to the product.
