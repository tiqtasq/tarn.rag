# Valve maintenance

A gate valve seals on a wedge that slides across the flow; its most common maintenance item is the
stem packing, which is tightened or replaced to stop stem leaks. A globe valve seals a disc against a
seat to throttle flow, and its wear shows up as a damaged seat that must be lapped or replaced. Do not
substitute a gate valve where throttling is required, because a partly open gate erodes quickly.
