# Reciprocating compressor startup

## Startup procedure

Before starting the compressor, confirm the suction and discharge valves are set to their startup
positions, open the cooling-water supply, and pre-lubricate the running gear by running the auxiliary
oil pump until the header pressure stabilises.

Then bar the machine over by hand to confirm it turns freely, start the driver unloaded, and bring the
unit up to speed before slowly loading each cylinder. Watch the lube-oil pressure and the cylinder
discharge temperatures throughout the loading sequence.

## Shutdown

To stop the compressor, unload the cylinders, stop the driver, and keep the auxiliary oil pump and the
cooling water running until the frame has cooled.
