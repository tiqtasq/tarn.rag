# Aboveground storage tank inspection

External and internal inspection of an aboveground storage tank follows the API 653 standard. The
external visual inspection is performed at least every 5 years, while the interval for ultrasonic
thickness (UT) measurement of the shell must not exceed the API 653 calculated interval, with an
upper bound of 15 years.

## Shell thickness

Measure shell thickness by ultrasonic testing (UT) at fixed locations on each course. Compare each
reading against the minimum required thickness to estimate the remaining corrosion allowance and the
next inspection due date.

## Foundation

Check the foundation and ringwall for settlement before returning the tank to service. A settlement
survey at eight points around the circumference detects differential settlement that can overstress
the shell-to-bottom weld.
